ABOUT THE SYSTEM

A simpler enrollment process

The GSCNSSAT Enrollment Enhancement System is a student-focused prototype for Grades 7–12. It gathers student, parent or guardian, and enrollment details in one clear, structured form for review by authorized school personnel.

  • Student details: name, date of birth, contact information, and previous school.
  • Enrollment details: applicant type, Grade 7–12, and school year.
  • Guardian details: parent or guardian name, relationship, and contact number.

This is a project prototype. Submitted information is not currently saved or sent to GSCNSSAT.
